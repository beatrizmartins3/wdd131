Soulmate concept beyond the romance

The concept of a soulmate extends far beyond romance, challenging the notion that such a connection exists solely for romantic love or marriage. Far removed from romantic comedy clichés, a soulmate represents a profound connection between two essences that aims, above all, for spiritual growth, healing, and personal evolution.

Example of a Soulmate Beyond Romance

Grey's Anatomy (Meredith and Cristina)

"You're my person"

Meredith and Cristina don't stroke each other's egos. They speak hard truths because they know that's what the other needs to hear and that it strengthens their friendship.
