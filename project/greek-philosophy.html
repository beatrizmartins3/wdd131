Soulmate concept in Greek Philosophy

The Myth of Split Souls — Plato

According to the myth, human beings were originally complete creatures with two faces, four arms, and four legs. These beings were very powerful and, according to the myth, attempted to challenge the gods. Zeus then decided to split them in half, thereby weakening them. After the split, each half began searching for its other half. Upon finding this lost part, they felt a deep desire to become one again.
