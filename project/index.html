The general concept

Soulmates is the idea that there are two people who share an especially deep and meaningful connection, as if they were naturally compatible on some level. A soulmate does not necessarily mean a perfect or conflict-free relationship. Even two highly compatible people can have differences and face difficulties, and they may need to work on building the relationship.

Put simply, it would be that person who makes you think: “It feels like this person understands me in a way few people can.”
