Soulmate concept in Spiritual Life

A soulmate, in the spiritual sense, is not necessarily the person who completes what is missing in you. It is someone whose presence awakens the best within you—and with whom you can journey toward goodness, truth, and God.

Example of a soulmate in Spiritual Life:

A Walk to Remember

In this movie, she doesn't fall in love with who he is, but with what she sees his soul can become. It is a spiritual gaze that sees beyond.
